Prismatic open-sourced its connector library under Apache-2.0, arguing that connector count is a vanity metric. The company contends the real value of an embedded iPaaS lies in platform capabilities like deployment at scale, monitoring, versioning, and operational support, not the number of pre-built connectors, especially as AI can now generate them on demand.
