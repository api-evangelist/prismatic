An embedded iPaaS platform accelerates integration deployment but cannot fix underlying product strategy issues. Organizations must address API maturity, integration prioritization, support processes, data architecture, and organizational ownership to maximize ROI from the platform. The article clarifies that while an embedded iPaaS removes infrastructure burden and enables faster shipping, it requires teams to bring their own integration strategy and product decisions to the table.
